# **What it is**

Drug Expansion adds a complete MDMA production chain to Schedule I. Meet a new supplier, source precursors, make crystals at the Chemistry Station, and press finished heart-shaped tablets with a Manual Tablet Press.

# **What it adds**

## **Disco Davey**

Disco Davey is a new Uptown supplier connected to Herbert Bleuball and Tobias Wentworth. Build enough rapport with either contact to receive a recommendation, then request a supplier meeting to order:

- Low-Grade Safrole
- Safrole
- High-Grade Safrole
- Methylamine

## **MDMA production**

Use one Safrole variant, one Methylamine, and one Acid at a Chemistry Station to make a batch of 10 MDMA crystals.

Crystals are then loaded into the Manual Tablet Press. Each completed press cycle turns one crystal into one pink, heart-shaped tablet.

Finished MDMA tablets have custom visuals and integrate with the game's normal product flow. They can be packaged in baggies or jars, consumed, mixed, discovered, listed in Product Manager, and saved with the game.

## **Manual Tablet Press**

The Manual Tablet Press is a floor-standing, hand-operated station sold by Handy Hank's Hardware and Dan's Hardware for $5,000 after reaching Baron I.

- Load one or more MDMA crystals into the press.
- Start the task, then rotate the visible wheel clockwise through three full turns.
- Each completed cycle consumes one crystal and creates one tablet.
- Employees can be assigned to operate the station.

# **How to start**

- Build enough rapport with Herbert Bleuball or Tobias Wentworth to unlock Disco Davey.
- Meet Davey, then order Safrole and Methylamine through his supplier menu.
- Make MDMA crystals at a Chemistry Station with one Safrole variant, one Methylamine, and one Acid.
- Buy and place a Manual Tablet Press.
- Load the crystals and press them into tablets.
- The first completed tablet discovers MDMA. Listing it for sale remains a separate Product Manager action.

## **Manual Tablet Press controls**

- **Mouse:** Rotate the visible wheel clockwise through three full turns.
- **Gamepad:** The press uses Schedule I's native station rotation input.

# **Basic install**

- Install **MelonLoader** for Schedule I.
- Install the matching runtime releases of [S1API (Forked)](https://www.nexusmods.com/schedule1/mods/1194) and **S1MAPI**.
- Download the Drug Expansion DLL that matches your game runtime.
- Put that DLL in Schedule I's `Mods` folder.
- Launch the game.

## **Important: pick the right file**

Install only the DLL that matches your game runtime.

- **IL2CPP:** `DrugExpansion_Il2cpp.dll`
- **Mono:** `DrugExpansion_Mono.dll`

# **Requirements**

- MelonLoader
- [S1API (Forked)](https://www.nexusmods.com/schedule1/mods/1194)
- S1MAPI

Drug Expansion ships separate Mono and IL2CPP builds.

# **Multiplayer note**

Every player in the session must use the same Drug Expansion release and matching dependency builds.

# **Current limitations**

- MDMA crystals are production intermediates. They cannot be packaged, consumed, sold to customers, or listed in Product Manager.
- Fresh saves do not start with MDMA discovered or listed. Press the first tablet to discover it, then list it manually.
- Existing saves with ambiguous MDMA discovery or listing state are preserved rather than rewritten.
